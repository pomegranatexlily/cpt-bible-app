Privacy & data

Oustory Bible · CPT–APE working edition · Effective October 19, 2026

Oustory is a Bible reading and study app published by the Pomegranate × Lily project. You can read without making an account. Your personal notes and reading choices are saved on your device, not to an Oustory user account or synchronization server.

No mandatory acceptance screen: Oustory currently has no advertising trackers, analytics SDK, or optional data-sharing feature that needs a blanket “I agree” gate. Reading does not depend on agreeing to marketing or tracking. This notice explains the app's actual data handling; optional actions remain under your control.

What stays on your device

Oustory uses browser local storage for bookmarks, last reading position, highlighted verses, personal verse notes, reading-plan and Sabbath study progress, calendar-track choice, reading appearance, and audio speed. The browser's Cache Storage and service worker hold downloaded Scripture, app files, and a copy of published calendar data to help the app work offline.

These values remain in the browser profile where you saved them. Oustory does not offer sign-in, cloud note syncing, or automatic cross-device backups. Clearing browser website data or uninstalling the app may remove them. On a shared device, someone with access to that browser profile could see saved notes.

What travels over the network

When you open Oustory, your browser requests app files, Scripture, search and research resources from its GitHub Pages website. Its Covenant Calendar also requests a public calendar feed from the related Shabbat Calendar project at pomegranatexlily.github.io/shabbat-calendar, when connected, with an offline fallback when available. Those requests can disclose ordinary connection metadata, such as an IP address and browser information, to the hosting provider. Oustory does not include your saved personal notes or bookmarks in those requests.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process request and security logs under its own policies. The Oustory application itself does not install an analytics package, advertising pixels, or first-party tracking cookies in the reviewed release. Browser or hosting-service behavior is outside the app's complete control.

Sharing, exporting, and importing

Sharing a verse or verse image uses your device's share interface only when you choose Share. The destination app or service you select then controls its copy. Export saved data creates a JSON file locally, including personal notes if you have them; keep this file somewhere you trust. Importing a backup requires file selection, format validation, and confirmation before saved values are replaced. Backups are not automatically uploaded to Oustory.

Your choices and deletion

Open Oustory → More → Offline reading & backup to export your saved data, delete only your locally saved reading and study data, or remove downloaded offline books. These are separate actions so deleting notes will not accidentally remove books, and deleting books will not silently erase notes. Your browser can also clear Oustory's website data entirely; that may remove all local notes and downloaded files.

Personal verse notes may reveal private beliefs or experiences. Oustory does not encrypt local storage with a separate password. Browser local storage is separated by web origin (scheme and hostname), not by the project folder. Other applications served from the same pomegranatexlily.github.io origin can technically access these saved keys. A dedicated Oustory hostname would offer stronger separation; until then, avoid highly sensitive notes, especially on shared or unsecured devices.

External links and changes

Links to manuscript sources or related projects may open other websites with their own privacy practices. Oustory may update this notice when features or data practices materially change. If optional analytics, accounts, or cloud synchronization are introduced later, their collection and choices must be explained before activation.

Questions

For non-sensitive questions about this published app, use the Oustory GitHub issues page. Issues are public: do not post personal notes, passwords, or identifying information there. There is currently no in-app account data to request from Oustory servers.
