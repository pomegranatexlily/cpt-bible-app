# cpt-bible-app
Covenant People's Translation (CPT) Bible App — After Passover Edition, by Dr. Nachamasha Yahsharala.

## 80-book Oustory reading edition — 2026-10-16

The app data now covers all **80** books in the founder's working sequence, **1,362** chapters and **36,801** existing source verses. The verified Genesis–Isaiah data files are preserved. **57** additional source books are loaded from traceable public-domain witnesses, including all **14** additional historical titles. For those fourteen titles, **5,390** verse positions now use a modern-English, public-domain World English Bible witness (with its ZIP and individual files SHA-256 recorded); **313** positions retain provisionally modernized historical 1611 wording where an equivalent modern position was not imported. Both witnesses are attributed separately. These are not claimed as a final unified translation. Verse-number omissions at Luke 17:36, Acts 8:37, Acts 15:34, Acts 24:7 and Ecclesiasticus 1:7, 17:5 remain expressly documented rather than filled or renumbered.

The main production manifest is [data/manifest.json](data/manifest.json). [The source build workflow](.github/workflows/build-full-reading-edition.yml) verifies the original 23 book checksums, imports the remaining 57 from pinned source revisions, collates the modern Deuterocanon/Apocrypha from a SHA-256-pinned WEB USFM archive, writes 80 per-book reader files and a searchable 80-book index, and runs structural integrity checks. Additional-book speaker colors are deferred as directed. This is a **complete editable source-based reading edition, not the final author-approved CPT–APE translation**. No change is made to the independent canonical 527-page PDF.

## Release 1 — mobile reading and search (October 2026)

Oustory preserves the verified 80-book working edition and adds reader-only improvements:

- **Continue Reading** tracks the user's current verse as they scroll, restoring the last book, chapter and verse after the app reopens. Existing chapter-only saved positions continue working.
- **Saved Places** opens reliably from Study and the More menu; existing saved chapters are preserved. A tap on a verse now offers **Save Verse** for precise bookmarks.
- **Search** downloads one on-demand full-text index, rather than requesting all 80 book JSON files sequentially. It supports case-insensitive words/phrases, exact references such as `Genesis 1:1`, 100 displayed matches, and responsive batched searching.
- **Text integrity:** `scripts/build_search_index.py` regenerates a disposable publication index from the exact SHA-256-verified per-book source files. It never changes verse text, numbering, colors, provenance, editorial notes, or the CPT–APE master PDF. Search data is created during deployment, not maintained as a second editable Scripture source.
- **Testing:** `scripts/test_release1.py` validates the complete 36,801-verse source index and preserves known witness omissions; the mobile Playwright test exercises saved verse actions, resume after reload, real menu routing, search behavior, and the two-track calendar.

Personal bookmarks and reading position remain **local to this browser** with no cloud sync or account system. Offline access, cross-device backup, editorial completion, and POM AI belong to separate future release phases.

## Release 2 — Research Bible (October 17, 2026)

The **Oustory working edition remains the main Scripture text**, unchanged in `data/<id>.json`. The Research Bible adds comparison and editorial examination without claiming that a provisional source verse is the founder's completed CPT–APE translation.

**Printed-English comparison.** Tap a Scripture verse and choose **Compare 1611**, or use **Compare 1611 KJV** below the chapter title to see entire chapters in two columns (stacked vertically on phones). Oustory's displayed wording and the pinned 1611 English KJV transcription appear next to each other, with exact word differences highlighted. These include spelling and punctuation differences: **a highlighted English difference is not evidence of a manuscript variant, mistranslation, or doctrinal drift by itself**. Both sides retain their actual chapter and verse coordinates, including visible gaps without inventing missing text or silently shifting positions. Source URLs and SHA checks are provided separately from theological interpretation.

The KJV transcription is generated by `scripts/build_kjv_comparison.py` at release time from the pinned `aruljohn/Bible-kjv-1611` revision `8ef066868ad1b7204d7be48658ea3bd1783d409b`, already used by Oustory for its historical 1611 witness. This is a **historical English printed edition**, not a critical Greek, Hebrew or Aramaic manuscript apparatus. Of the 80 titles, 79 have a mapped source file. **The Rest of Esther** has no independently verified matching separate title with the exact 10:4–16 verse coordinates in this source repository; its comparison says unavailable rather than substituting standard Esther 1–10.

**Editorial research.** `data/editorial-notes.json` retains the founder rulings and unresolved existing notes and adds the **October 17 founder correction** on `ethnos / ethnē` / Gentiles. Greek `ethnos` means nation/people; context distinguishes an Israelite nation (Luke 7:5), non-Israelite nations contrasted with Israel (Acts 4:27), and broad groupings (Matthew 28:19). The previous draft directive to replace every `Gentiles` with `nations` as if the referents were automatically identical or automatically non-Israelite is **superseded**. No global replacement is permitted: every verse must be reviewed individually and justified by its context. This correction is an editorial rule, **not a new KJV or manuscript text**.

`data/terminology-review.json` preserves **27** actual wording-review categories from pages 36–38 of the October 16 working draft (which incorrectly labels the total as 25). All categories except the founder's specific contextual correction remain **unapproved draft review prompts**. `data/witness-questions.json` records 15 identified textual questions, **not 15 resolved variants**; source ages, attestation, and individual manuscript readings still require independent verification, with no automatic preference for a Masoretic reading. The older **63-note apparatus** remains a draft source; the app does **not** claim that all 63 have been reconciled, individually checked, or author-approved.

**Engineering:** comparison JSON is generated under `data/compare/` **only in the release artifact**, not committed as an editable competing Bible text. The manuscript QC checks 80 source book checksums and original verse coverage; `scripts/test_release2.py` checks review states, the source correction, and pinned comparison alignment after generation. Mobile browser tests cover the new Research UI and the existing saved reading/search/calendar flows. No POM AI integration is added in this release.
