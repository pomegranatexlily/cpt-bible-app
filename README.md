# cpt-bible-app
Covenant People's Translation (CPT) Bible App — After Passover Edition, by Dr. Nachamasha Yahsharala.

## 80-book Oustory reading edition — 2026-10-16

The app data now covers all **80** books in the founder's working sequence, **1,362** chapters and **36,801** existing source verses. The verified Genesis–Isaiah data files are preserved. **57** additional source books are loaded from traceable public-domain witnesses, including all **14** additional historical titles. For those fourteen titles, **5,390** verse positions now use a modern-English, public-domain World English Bible witness (with its ZIP and individual files SHA-256 recorded); **313** positions retain provisionally modernized historical 1611 wording where an equivalent modern position was not imported. Both witnesses are attributed separately. These are not claimed as a final unified translation. Verse-number omissions at Luke 17:36, Acts 8:37, Acts 15:34, Acts 24:7 and Ecclesiasticus 1:7, 17:5 remain expressly documented rather than filled or renumbered.

The main production manifest is [data/manifest.json](data/manifest.json). [The source build workflow](.github/workflows/build-full-reading-edition.yml) verifies the original 23 book checksums, imports the remaining 57 from pinned source revisions, collates the modern Deuterocanon/Apocrypha from a SHA-256-pinned WEB USFM archive, writes 80 per-book reader files and a searchable 80-book index, and runs structural integrity checks. Additional-book speaker colors are deferred as directed. This is a **complete editable source-based reading edition, not the final author-approved CPT–APE translation**. No change is made to the independent canonical 527-page PDF.

## Release 1 — mobile reading and search (October 2026)

Oustory preserves the verified 80-book working edition and adds reader-only improvements:

- **Continue Reading** tracks the user's current verse as they scroll, restoring the last book, chapter and verse after the app reopens. Existing chapter-only saved positions continue working.
- **Saved Places** opens reliably from Study and the More menu; existing saved chapters are preserved. A tap on a verse now offers **Save Verse** for precise bookmarks.
- **Search** downloads one on-demand full-text index, rather than requesting all 80 book JSON files sequentially. It supports case-insensitive words/phrases, exact references such as `Genesis 1:1`, 100 displayed matches, and responsive batched searching.
- **Text integrity:** `scripts/build_search_index.py` regenerates a disposable publication index from the exact SHA-256-verified per-book source files. It never changes verse text, numbering, colors, provenance, editorial notes, or the CPT–APE master PDF. Search data is created during deployment, not maintained as a second editable Scripture source.
- **Testing:** `scripts/test_release1.py` validates the complete 36,801-verse source index and preserves known witness omissions; the mobile Playwright test exercises saved verse actions, resume after reload, real menu routing, search behavior, and the two-track calendar.

Personal bookmarks and reading position remain **local to this browser** with no cloud sync or account system. Offline access, cross-device backup, editorial completion, and POM AI belong to separate future release phases.

## Release 2 — Research Bible (October 17, 2026)

The **Oustory working edition remains the main Scripture text**, unchanged in `data/<id>.json`. The Research Bible adds comparison and editorial examination without claiming that a provisional source verse is the founder's completed CPT–APE translation.

**Printed-English comparison.** Tap a Scripture verse and choose **Compare 1611**, or use **Compare 1611 KJV** below the chapter title to see entire chapters in two columns (stacked vertically on phones). Oustory's displayed wording and the pinned 1611 English KJV transcription appear next to each other, with exact word differences highlighted. These include spelling and punctuation differences: **a highlighted English difference is not evidence of a manuscript variant, mistranslation, or doctrinal drift by itself**. Both sides retain their actual chapter and verse coordinates, including visible gaps without inventing missing text or silently shifting positions. Source URLs and SHA checks are provided separately from theological interpretation.

The KJV transcription is generated by `scripts/build_kjv_comparison.py` at release time from the pinned `aruljohn/Bible-kjv-1611` revision `8ef066868ad1b7204d7be48658ea3bd1783d409b`, already used by Oustory for its historical 1611 witness. This is a **historical English printed edition**, not a critical Greek, Hebrew or Aramaic manuscript apparatus. Of the 80 titles, 79 have a mapped source file. **The Rest of Esther** has no independently verified matching separate title with the exact 10:4–16 verse coordinates in this source repository; its comparison says unavailable rather than substituting standard Esther 1–10.

**Editorial research.** `data/editorial-notes.json` retains the founder rulings and unresolved existing notes and adds the **October 17 founder correction** on `ethnos / ethnē` / Gentiles. Greek `ethnos` means nation/people; context distinguishes an Israelite nation (Luke 7:5), non-Israelite nations contrasted with Israel (Acts 4:27), and broad groupings (Matthew 28:19). The October 16 draft already recognizes that Israel itself can be described as a nation. The founder's October 17 clarification explicitly distinguishes this **lexical preference** from the **referent**: the word does not always mean non-Israelites, although some passages clearly refer to non-Israelites. No unreviewed mass replacement is permitted: every proposed main-text change must be justified by grammar and local context. This correction is an editorial rule, **not a new KJV or manuscript text**.

`data/terminology-review.json` preserves **27** actual wording-review categories from pages 36–38 of the October 16 working draft (which incorrectly labels the total as 25). All categories except the founder's specific contextual correction remain **unapproved draft review prompts**. `data/witness-questions.json` records 15 identified textual questions, **not 15 resolved variants**; source ages, attestation, and individual manuscript readings still require independent verification, with no automatic preference for a Masoretic reading. The older **63-note apparatus** remains a draft source; the app does **not** claim that all 63 have been reconciled, individually checked, or author-approved.

**Engineering:** comparison JSON is generated under `data/compare/` **only in the release artifact**, not committed as an editable competing Bible text. The manuscript QC checks 80 source book checksums and original verse coverage; `scripts/test_release2.py` checks review states, the source correction, and pinned comparison alignment after generation. Mobile browser tests cover the new Research UI and the existing saved reading/search/calendar flows. No POM AI integration is added in this release.

## Release 3 — Covenant Calendar and Study (October 17, 2026)

The Oustory calendar connects **only to the Shabbat Calendar engine's published date feed**. Its two visible modes are **Early Start** and **Late Start**, with founder-designated 2027 Month 1 dates of March 9 and April 7. A selected civil date remains selected when switching traditions. The engine audits lunar arithmetic and publishes a machine-readable `verification` status: supplied 2026 dates and future mean-lunation projections are **not independently verified crescent sightings, observed local sunset boundaries, or proven Abib**. No third calendar is invented.

A new `data/covenant-study.json` curates **Scripture references** (never substituted Bible text) for the four lunar Sabbath days, the New Moon, Sabbath preparation, and the ten existing draft feast names. These are *suggested study readings*, not founder-approved festival rules or an obligatory lectionary. Users can access readings under the selected calendar day, and the next Sabbath's study from Home, Study, and Discover. Each Read button opens the exact Oustory book/chapter/verse. Mark-read progress is stored **locally and independently** by civil date, selected track, and passage.

Draft feast dates still display `unapproved_draft`. **Shavuot has no calculated day** pending a founder-approved count; its supporting passages are offered in an undated research section. The thirteenth-month seasonal model is not a Jubilee, and no makeup feast is automatically doubled because two year-start traditions exist.

`scripts/test_release3.py` validates each linked Scripture reference against the actual 80-book source JSON, including non-contiguous verse numbering. Mobile tests check October 19, 2026 (supplied Month Day 8 Shabbat), the three associated Day 8 readings, track-separated completion marks, working verse navigation, and the audit disclosure. Release 3 changes no CPT–APE manuscript or AI implementation.

## Release 4 — Offline reading and publication preparation (October 17, 2026)

**More → Offline reading & backup** provides an optional one-time download of the 80-book working text for reading without a network connection. A first-party service worker caches the app's interface and Genesis; other source books are cached as opened or downloaded. Progress and cancellation are shown in the app. The **Remove downloaded books** control leaves saved study data untouched; offline cache is local to the device and subject to browser storage eviction.

**Export saved data** creates an Oustory JSON v1 file containing the reader's saved places/verse position, highlights, reading-plan and Sabbath study completion, selected calendar track, and reading/audio settings. **Import backup file** validates the format and Scripture references, requests explicit confirmation before replacing those fields, then reloads to refresh every saved-data view. Neither backup nor offline downloads transmit saved personal study data to the server. There is no login, cloud sync, or automatic multi-device backup.

Accessibility refinements include clearer chapter-change announcements, loading states, keyboard focus, higher contrast, reduced-motion handling, and live download/restore feedback. The CI browser test exercises a full 80-book download, airplane-mode-equivalent browser reload and chapter reading, backup round-trip, and malformed import rejection. See [Release 4 publication checklist](docs/RELEASE4_OFFLINE_PUBLICATION_2026-10-17.md).

**Publication status:** web/PWA distribution is prepared and testable, but no native app-store submission or founder approval of the final CPT–APE manuscript is implied. Provisional source witness status remains explicitly unchanged. Live Shabbat calendar data and unvisited comparison/search files may still need a network connection. POM AI remains outside the Release 4 scope.

## Release 5 — Local verse image cards (October 17, 2026)

Tap any Scripture verse and select **Create verse image** to render an actual 1080px-wide PNG image from the exact text already loaded in Oustory. The image has the real book/chapter/verse reference and a visible **CPT–APE · Oustory Working Edition** and **editorial review ongoing** label; it does not claim an author-approved final translation. The client renders images with local Canvas primitives and system fonts, with no stock photos, image-generation API, tracking requests, backend account, or altered source text.

A modal previews the image, offers **Share PNG** through the device's file-sharing sheet when supported and **Save PNG** through the browser's native download behavior. If file-sharing is unsupported, it explains the limitation and keeps Save PNG available. Extremely long source verses are explicitly refused rather than cropped or silently shortened; the normal plain-text **Share verse** action remains available. Image generation does not change saved verses, the 80-book canon, provenance, comparison witnesses or the CPT–APE manuscript.

Offline support: the already existing Oustory service worker precaches `oustory-share-card.js` and `oustory-share-card.css` and rotates its shell version. A real-browser `scripts/share_card_ui.mjs` test validates PNG header bytes/dimensions, reference/edition disclosure, accessible image description, download and failure handling. Physical iPhone Safari sharing/save remains a post-deployment device check.

## Release 6 — Phone-first study workbench (October 17, 2026)

A Scripture verse now opens a concise mobile action sheet with **Compare · Interlinear · Notes**. Ancillary tools (plain-text share, PNG sharing, bookmark, highlight and editorial research) are preserved in a collapsed **More verse actions** section. Selecting a primary tool opens a dedicated distraction-free study page, not a stack of nested popups. **Back to Scripture** returns to the original reader and scroll position. A chapter heading's **Compare versions** shortcut opens the complete 1611 chapter comparison using the original verse coordinates and explicit missing-verse status.

**Compare** uses only the SHA-pinned 1611 printed-English KJV already published. No other edition is suggested as available. No English spelling difference is presented as a manuscript variant.

**Personal Notes** stores observations by immutable `book-id|chapter|verse` keys under `oustory.personal-notes.v1`, explicitly separate from the founder's editorial notes. The existing offline JSON backup/export system now validates, exports, and restores those notes alongside bookmarks and highlights without changing legacy backup format version 1. Local browser storage is subject to device storage limits and can be cleared by the browser.

**Interlinear** has a dedicated, prepared reader and a fail-closed `data/interlinear/manifest.json` with **zero** source books. This is an honest feature foundation, **not yet a functioning original-language interlinear**: original Hebrew/Greek tokens, English alignments, Strong’s numbers, grammars, lexicon definitions and occurrence lists are unavailable until independently pinned and validated source datasets are acquired. The app refuses to derive such details from English or fabricate them for show. Unversified texts and books lacking alignment are clearly unavailable.

The existing 80-book manuscript, 1611 witness and calendar sources are unchanged. `scripts/workbench_ui.mjs` tests real phone-sized navigation, verified comparison, no fabricated interlinear entries, personal notes across reload, valid backup/restore, and invalid backup rejection. Together with the existing mobile smoke tests, this release is an incremental research/navigation upgrade, not a completed Blue Letter Bible replacement. The 19 additional Scripture candidates remain on the separate Perplexity/CPT–APE source-acquisition lane.
